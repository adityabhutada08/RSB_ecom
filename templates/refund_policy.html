Refund & Return Policy

1. Overview

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you are not happy with a product, our refund and return policy is designed to be fair, transparent, and customer-friendly, while also considering the nature of perishable goods.

2. Eligibility for Returns & Refunds

  • Perishable Items: Returns are accepted within 24 hours of delivery/pickup if the product is spoiled, damaged, or not as described. Please contact us immediately with photos and details.
  • Non-Perishable Items: Returns are accepted within 7 days of delivery/pickup, provided the item is unused, unopened, and in its original packaging.
  • Receipt Required: Please provide your order confirmation or receipt for all returns and refund requests.

3. How to Request a Return or Refund

  1. Email us at rsb.grocery@gmail.com with your order number, product details, reason for return, and supporting photos (if applicable).
  2. Alternatively, call us at +91 9422796555 during business hours.
  3. Our team will review your request and provide instructions for return or next steps.

4. Refund Process & Timelines

  • Once your return is received and inspected, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund.
  • Approved refunds will be processed within 5-7 business days to your original payment method.
  • For cash on delivery orders, refunds will be issued as store credit or via bank transfer.

5. Exchanges

We offer exchanges for eligible items if you receive a defective or incorrect product. Please contact us within the return window to arrange an exchange.

6. Exceptions & Non-Returnable Items

  • Products that are opened, used, or not in their original condition are not eligible for return.
  • Perishable items purchased in excess or by mistake (wrong variety, flavor, size, etc.) are not eligible for return due to food safety and environmental concerns.
  • Gift cards, promotional items, and items marked as "final sale" are non-returnable.

7. Customer Rights & Dispute Resolution

If you are dissatisfied with our resolution, please contact us directly. We are committed to resolving all issues fairly and promptly.

8. Contact Information

  • Email: rsb.grocery@gmail.com
  • Phone: +91 9422796555
  • Visit our Contact Us page for more options.

We reserve the right to update this policy at any time. Please review this page regularly for any changes.
